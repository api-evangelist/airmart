# Airmart

> Airmart (goairmart.com) is a Redwood City, California e-commerce platform for small sellers — home bakers, farms, restaurants, cottage-food makers, creators and community group buys — who open a link-based online storefront, take orders and payments, and fulfil through pickup, shipping, DoorDash on-demand delivery or Airmart's own route-planned delivery.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-19 from the apis.yml profile and the artifacts in this repository. Airmart publishes no public developer program, API documentation, SDK, webhooks or machine-readable API contract; api.goairmart.com and ts-api.goairmart.com are private first-party backends for its own web and mobile apps. Do not attempt to call them.
